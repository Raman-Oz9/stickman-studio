The rocket rose from the earth in a storm of fire and smoke.
For seven months the astronauts drifted through the silent dark of space.
At last, the red planet appeared, glowing in the distance.
They landed on Mars, and the astronaut stepped onto the dust.
"That's one small step," she whispered, "for a new home."
The wind blew red sand across the lonely desert as the crew walked toward a giant canyon.
At night, the stars were brighter than anyone on earth had ever seen.
A day on Mars lasts almost the same as a day on Earth, only thirty-seven minutes longer.
