The diver sank slowly beneath the waves into the blue.
Sunlight faded, and the water turned dark as night.
A huge whale glided past, singing in the deep.
Far below, the submarine descended toward the seabed, past glowing fish.
"We are deeper than any person has ever been," the captain said.
At the bottom of the Mariana Trench, the pressure is over one thousand times higher than at the surface.
Strange creatures lived there, in total darkness, far from the sun.
