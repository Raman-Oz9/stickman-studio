Alexander led his army across the sea at dawn.
At last the fleet reached the shore, and his soldiers stepped onto the sand.
In Egypt, beneath the great pyramids, a queen was waiting for him.
She smiled and said, "Welcome to Egypt. Gold is only sand that someone loved."
But the Persians marched against him, and the two armies clashed on the plain in a storm.
Victorious, Alexander knelt, and the queen placed the golden crown upon his head.
In the marble halls of the temple, the council gathered to plan the future of the empire.
Then the army marched across the burning desert, through blowing sand.
From a high cliff, the king looked over his empire, and eagles flew below him.
Alexander died young, yet his empire changed the world forever.
