A mango seller opened his little shop at dawn and filled the shelves with golden mangoes. A thief crept in, grabbed a bag of coins from the counter and jumped on a horse. The cowboy sheriff chased him in a roaring red car past a huge mountain. Thunder cracked, rain poured, and the thief fled into a dark gold mine. Inside the mine, he found a mountain of glittering gold. Then the king walked into his great palace and ordered the guards to catch him. A pirate ship sailed in the harbor, and a helicopter flew over the sea. In the end the king laughed, and everyone cheered.
