Alexander led his army across the blue sea at dawn.
The bronze fleet reached the dry shore, and soldiers stepped onto hot sand.
In Egypt, beneath limestone pyramids, a queen waited inside a cool stone palace.
She smiled and whispered, "Welcome to the throne of pharaohs."
Persian armies marched across the dusty plain during a violent sandstorm.
Spears clashed under dark storm clouds while war drums beat.
Victorious, Alexander raised a golden crown toward the setting sun.
He founded twenty cities named Alexandria across the vast empire.
Yet history records a surprising fact.
Alexander never lost a single battle throughout fifteen years of continuous conquest.
