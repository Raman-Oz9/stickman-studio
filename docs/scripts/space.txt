The silver rocket rose from the launchpad in a blinding flash at midnight.
For seven months, astronauts drifted through the silent dark of deep space.
At last, a rusty red planet appeared glowing on the distant horizon.
The crew landed softly upon the cold rocky Martian dust.
The commander stepped out and declared, "One small step for human survival."
Violent red dust storms whipped across the cratered desert during the afternoon.
At night, two tiny irregular moons shone above jagged mountain ridges.
Scientists revealed a surprising fact.
Sunsets on Mars glow a striking blue instead of red.
